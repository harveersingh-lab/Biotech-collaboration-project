About the Site

"You can't connect the dots looking forward; you can only connect them looking backwards. So you have to trust that the dots will somehow connect in your future." - Steve Jobs
Discover Sciderizer Find Your Pathway

Finding the right career path is no easy task, especially not for high schoolers. Much less for high schoolers in science. Often times, these students struggle to decide on a path to walk for their career for any number of limitations. Maybe they know what they want but are unsure how to get there. Maybe they know the field they're passionate about but unsure about the options within it. Maybe they're completely lost.

Sciderizer understands that. And the leaders behind it want to help.

The leaders behind the site were in the same situation once before, and worked to find a way to help lost students find their way. These leaders and their partners worked to create Sciderizer, a tool that narrows down the interests, strengths, and weaknesses of students in stem. As Steve Jobs implied, connecting these dots from one's past is a crucial step in understanding your career path. Sciderizer provides insight on its user's future in science, acting as a first step for them in setting and achieving their goals.

Through a personalized test, the student can get an idea for their future, and it all starts with....
... a sign-up screen.
